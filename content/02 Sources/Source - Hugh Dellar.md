**Who:** Teacher and teacher trainer (University of Westminster); co-author with Andrew Walkley of *Outcomes* and *Innovations* and of *Teaching Lexically* (Delta Publishing). Mentored by Michael Lewis and Jimmie Hill. CTEFLA, DTEFLA, MA TESOL.

**What:** "Thoughts, rants and ramblings…", including the 2013 *Twenty things in twenty years* series and a Dogme/coursebook series. Closed January 2015; blogging continued at Lexical Lab.

**Stance:** Language is "at heart lexically driven". He critiques PPP, tense-syllabus grammar and recipe methodology, and makes a theory-first argument from corpora and priming (Hoey). He is pragmatic about coursebooks (as an author of them) and about Dogme (a middle ground).

**Read critically:** Polemical; key claims are often uncited.
