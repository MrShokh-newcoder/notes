**Who:** David Weller, a teacher (Trinity DipTESOL) and author of *Lesson Planning for Language Teachers*, *AI for Busy Teachers*, *Essential Classroom Management*, and other titles. Sells DipTESOL and DELTA Anki decks.

**What:** A Substack of crisp, practitioner explainers on core concepts and techniques.

**Stance:** Pragmatic and terminology-precise. Takes SLA seriously but rejects purist versions ("CI is necessary but not sufficient"). Light on citations.
