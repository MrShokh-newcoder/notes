**Who:** Jim Scrivener (*Learning Teaching*, *Classroom Management Techniques*) and Adrian Underhill (*Sound Foundations*), with guest posts.

**What:** The companion blog to their Demand High seminars (launched at IATEFL 2012). Conversation moved to Facebook in 2014.

**Stance:** A "meme, not a method": a small course correction asking teachers to push each learner to their next doable step, instead of settling for completed tasks and "covering material". It works with CLT, Dogme or coursebooks.

## Processed notes
- [[Doable demand at the learner's next step]]
- [[One-to-one-in-a-group - stop rubberstamping answers]]
- [[Prepare a bank of challenge prompts]]
- [[Upgrade language during fluency work with minimal interference]]
