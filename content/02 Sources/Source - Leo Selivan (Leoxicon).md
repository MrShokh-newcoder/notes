**Who:** Teacher trainer and lexical-approach practitioner (ex-British Council). His book *Lexical Grammar* (CUP) is referenced on the blog. Authorship not confirmed from the pages read.

**What:** "Activities, ideas and useful tips with a lexical touch", plus reflections and rants. Busiest year: 2013.

**Stance:** Lexico-grammatical: words come with collocations and colligations; grammar works bottom-up from patterns; L1 and translation are deliberate tools. Explicit grammar can coexist.

**Useful bibliography of Lexical Approach critics** ([post](https://leoxicon.blogspot.com/2016/02/lexical-approach-criticism.html)): Thornbury 1998 (*MET* 7), Lindstromberg 2003 (*HLT* 5/2), Swan 2006 (*Teacher Trainer* 20/3), Timmis 2008 (*MET* 17/3), Walter 2012, Scheffler 2015 (*ELTJ* 69/1). Thornbury confirms in the comments that "all chunks and no pineapple" is his phrase.
