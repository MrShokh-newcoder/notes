**Who:** Former Head of MFL (24 years, Ripon Grammar); MA in SLA (Institute of Education); co-author with Conti of *The Language Teacher Toolkit*, *Breaking the Sound Barrier* (2019) and *Memory* (2021); author of *Becoming an Outstanding Languages Teacher* and *Thinking About Language Teaching* (2025).

**What:** Research commentary, book reviews and classroom ideas. Mostly UK MFL policy (GCSE), so a lot of it is irrelevant to EFL. The listening and Nation posts transfer well.

**Stance vs Conti:** Same camp, different emphasis. Smith weights comprehensible input and communicative interaction more, with micro-listening as a supplement. He would downplay analysis at A1–A2.
