**Who:** PhD Applied Linguistics (Oxford, supervised by Ernesto Macaro; thesis on metacognitive strategies in L2 writing). Former head of MFL in UK secondary schools. Co-author with Steve Smith of *The Language Teacher Toolkit*, *Breaking the Sound Barrier* and *Memory: What Every Language Teacher Should Know*. Creator of **E.P.I.** (Extensive Processing Instruction) and Sentence Builders; founder of language-gym.com.

**What:** ~300+ posts, very active (several a month in 2026). Weak categorisation, so the "Top Posts" sidebar is the best way in.

**Stance:** Lexicogrammar-first, input-before-output, heavily recycled. Draws on skill-acquisition theory (DeKeyser), input processing (VanPatten), Processability (Pienemann), output and feedback (Swain, Lyster), Nation's fluency strand, cognitive load. Polemical about "evidence-informed" general-education fads being transferred to language teaching without ISLA scrutiny.

**Read critically:** Written for UK MFL (low exposure, 2–3 lessons a week). Promotes his own products. Some numbers are his own synthesis rather than research findings.
