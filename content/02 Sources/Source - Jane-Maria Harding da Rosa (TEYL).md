**Who:** YL teacher and trainer (Portugal), APPI presenter, OUP webinar and resource author. Now at Enriching ELT / EELT Academy ([new blog](https://enriching-elt.com/blog/)).

**What:** Practical YL activity write-ups: songs, chants, mini-books, the silent period, PBL, classroom instructions. Many handouts are PDFs (not processed).

**Stance:** Input-rich, meaning-first, rhythm-driven. Language arrives in chunks via songs, chants, stories and actions; she respects the silent period. Theory is light, so read the claims as practitioner experience.
