**Who:** Teacher trainer and materials writer (*Inside Out*, *Straightforward*, *Evolve*); author of *Translation and Own-Language Activities* (CUP) and *30 Trends in ELT*.

**What:** Started as a 10-part guide to adaptive learning (2014, later an ebook from The Round). It now covers gamification, data, vocabulary apps and generative AI.

**Stance:** A sceptical insider. Ask what problem a technology solves and what evidence supports it; read vendor and funder claims as marketing; keep teacher judgement central. Not anti-tech.
