"Being an eternal student"

Some of you may or may not have heard about the concept of being a life-long learner, being an eternal student in other words. Most of you might have read the quote that goes: "Never stop learning, because life never stops teaching..." or something like that, right? Some of my friends used to put it on their social media accounts, on Telegram profiles...
Even though they don't learn a thing on a daily basis. Kind of strange, isn't it?

I have a quick question: At what age do people stop learning whatever? At 22,23 - the age of undergraduated students or 24 when we'll have Master's Degree... What about people who weren't admitted by universities... huh? Can't we just sit and relax at home, watching Netflix... 
In this fast-paced world, the answer would be "Never" We have to upgrade and update our knowledge. Why exactly should we be a person who doesn't stop learning something new everyday? Why is this case?

According to statistical reports, young generations have to change or adapt to their own careers because of new types of innovations in any fields of our lives. There are plenty of brand new professions which didn't exist 5 years ago. Marketolog, Project Manager, copywriter... you name others.
