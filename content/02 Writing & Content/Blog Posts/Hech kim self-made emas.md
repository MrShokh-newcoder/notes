There is no such thing as a “self-made” man. We are made up of thousands of others. Everyone who has ever done a kind deed for us, or spoken one word of encouragement to us, has entered into the make-up of our character and of our thoughts, as well as our success. —George Burton Adams (Location 305)

Ijtimoiy tarmoqlarda ko'rib qolamiz:

Man shuncha yil o'qidim, izlandim, xarakat qildim va bugungi natijalarni qo'lga kiritdim

Hammasiga o'zim erishdim, o'z harakatlarim tufayli...

Bu insonlar o'zlarini ham, bizni ham aldayapti

Hech kim bir o'zi biror natijaga erisha olmaydi

Maslahat, tavsiya, motivatsiya, insayt...
