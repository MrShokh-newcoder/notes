## Where the sources disagree
- ⚠️ Most of Millin's exam-technique material predates the 2015 format change. Verify every structural detail against the current Cambridge handbook.
- **Module 1 task numbering conflicts:** Swift (2012), Pinard (2013) and Gordyshevskaya (2018) describe different task orders, timings and mark splits, and Millin's layout is pre-2015. Treat all three as *method* sources, never as format sources.
