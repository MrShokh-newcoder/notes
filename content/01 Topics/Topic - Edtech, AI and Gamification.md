What the evidence says about adaptive learning, gamification, vocabulary apps and generative AI.

## Where the sources disagree
- **Gamification evidence:** the British Council Indonesia evaluation reports teachers adopting gamification (self-report). Kerr and Kohn argue points and leaderboards give short-lived extrinsic motivation. Neither measured learning outcomes.
- **AI and learning:** Deng et al. (2024) found performance gains; Kerr says performance ≠ learning, and Stadler et al. (2024) found reduced mental effort and depth.
