When and how learners' own languages (Uzbek, Russian) help English learning. The field has moved from target-language-only orthodoxy to *planned* L1 use.

## Where the sources disagree
- **L2-only vs L1 support:** the ELT Research Bites teaser reports L2-only vocabulary explanations beat L1 use. Selivan, G. Cook and the MultiEd study support planned translation and translanguaging. These are compatible if L1 is a *planned scaffold*, not the default medium. *(Claude)*
