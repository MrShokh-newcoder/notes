Retention depends on spaced, effortful retrieval and on repeated encounters in varied contexts. Working memory limits how much is new per lesson.

## Where the sources disagree
- How much new language per lesson: Conti gives explicit numbers, but they are his synthesis rather than study findings.
- Neuroscience claims vs behavioural evidence: cite the latter for DELTA.
