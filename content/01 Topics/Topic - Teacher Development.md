CPD works when it is matched to the teacher and gets protected time.

## Where the sources disagree
- **Research vs craft knowledge:** Jordan says trainers must teach the SLA research on interlanguage; Thornbury (commenting) says experience is a legitimate basis and research is "ancillary". Dellar ("nothing as practical as a good theory") sits closer to Jordan, Ur closer to Thornbury.
- **Observation:** Griffin and TDSIG: separate developmental observation from evaluation and describe before judging. Most school observation systems mix the two.
- **What counts as PD:** Loseva and Polosatova count informal, unplanned learning; course-and-certificate checklists don't.
