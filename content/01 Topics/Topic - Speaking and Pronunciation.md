Speaking tasks, fluency, communication strategies and pronunciation (intelligibility, prosody, individual sounds). For exam classes this is where PET/FCE Speaking and IELTS Speaking preparation lives.

## Where the sources disagree
- **Accuracy vs identity:** Tichá and Bryan-Jordan (via Jennifer) argue for keeping L1-shaped English and learner varieties; Jennifer's own pronunciation posts work on individual sounds for B2+ professionals. Compatible if the target is *intelligibility for the learner's purpose* (Jenkins; Levis's intelligibility principle). *(Claude)*
- **Shadowing:** promoted as a speaking technique; the research mostly shows listening/decoding gains. *(Claude)*
