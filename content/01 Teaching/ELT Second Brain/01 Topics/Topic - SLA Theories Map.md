A one-page orientation for DELTA Module 1. Each row links to the notes where the theory does real work. The key-figure column is standard textbook attribution *(Claude)*. Check dates against Lightbown & Spada, *How Languages are Learned*, or Mitchell, Myles & Marsden, *Second Language Learning Theories*.
