Children learn through routines, relationships, stories and purposeful activity, within their ZPD. Affect and management are preconditions for learning, not extras.

## Where the sources disagree
- **Rhythm vs meaning first:** Harding da Rosa leads with chants and songs (phonology, chunks); Read with relationships, ZPD and stories. Complementary layers of the same lesson.
