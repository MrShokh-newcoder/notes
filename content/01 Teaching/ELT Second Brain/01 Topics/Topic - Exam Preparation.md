Preparing learners for Cambridge YL, KET/PET/FCE for Schools and IELTS without collapsing teaching into test practice. The organising idea is to use the criteria and past papers as *diagnostic* and *teaching* tools.

## Where the sources disagree
- **Washback:** criteria-led teaching (Cambridge posts) vs the risk of performance-without-acquisition ([[The illusion of learning - correct answers are not acquisition]]). The resolution is to teach the construct the criterion describes, not the task format. *(Claude)*
