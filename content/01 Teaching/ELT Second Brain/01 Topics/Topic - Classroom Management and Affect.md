Most behaviour problems are prevented by planning, pitch and consistent routines rather than handled by sanctions. Praise and rewards are powerful and double-edged.

## Where the sources disagree
- Rewards: Read lists reward systems as optional ("if used at all"), and descriptive praise warns against dependency. This matters for Tanga design. Read Alfie Kohn (bookmarked) next.
- **Praise:** Read endorses descriptive praise and CBG; Kohn rejects praise as control ("say what you saw" instead). The overlap is *describe, don't evaluate*. They disagree on whether praise should be a management tool at all.
- **Discipline:** Read's pre-emption is practical; Kohn's "beyond discipline" questions the goal of compliance itself.
