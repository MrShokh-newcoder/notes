How rewards, praise, grades, competition and homework affect motivation and learning. This is the evidence base for gamification and conduct systems (Tanga, Axloqiy baholash).

## Where the sources disagree
- **Kohn vs the meta-analyses:** Kohn says rewards undermine interest, full stop. Cameron & Pierce (1994) found small overall effects and positive effects of verbal rewards. Deci, Koestner & Ryan (1999) found *expected tangible contingent* rewards undermine free-choice intrinsic motivation, while unexpected rewards and positive feedback do not. Synthesis: the reward's *type, expectancy and contingency* matter more than its existence. *(Claude)*
- **Praise:** Read (descriptive praise as a management tool) vs Kohn (praise as control). Henderlong & Lepper (2002): informational, sincere, process praise can help.
- **Homework:** Kohn says no benefit before high school; Cooper et al. (2006) found positive associations at secondary.
- **Grit:** Kohn's scepticism is supported by Credé et al. (2017).
