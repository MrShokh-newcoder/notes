Meaning-first approaches: the task (TBLT) or the conversation (Dogme) drives the lesson, and form is attended to as it arises.

## Where the sources disagree
- Thornbury: same philosophy, different methodology. TBLT has research without practice; Dogme the reverse.
- Conti is a counter-position: structured, pre-selected input and production *before* spontaneity.
