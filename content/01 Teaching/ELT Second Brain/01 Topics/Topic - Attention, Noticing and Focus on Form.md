Input becomes intake only when it is attended to (Schmidt). The teaching questions are **when** to direct attention to form (proactively vs reactively) and **how** (enhancement, recasts, prompts, explicit rules).

## Where the sources disagree
- **Krashen vs Schmidt** plays out live in Thornbury's comment threads: is attention necessary, or just efficient?
- **Recasts vs prompts:** Doughty & Varela found durable gains from recasts, but recasts can be heard as agreement (Lyster).
