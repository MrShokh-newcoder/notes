> Dave Willis's The Lexical Syllabus (1990) and the COBUILD English Course started from the most frequent words (do, get, it, way), three years before Lewis's Lexical Approach (1993).

## Key points
- The COBUILD corpus showed "the enormous power of the common words of English" (Willis 1990: 46). The post cites ~700 words covering ~70% of text.
- English is minimally inflected, so "word meaning and word order are central" (Willis 1990: 23).
- The passive, second conditional and reported speech are better treated lexically than as big structures.
- Thornbury's *Natural Grammar* (OUP 2005) is built on Willis's "small words".

## Limits
A tribute, not a critique.
