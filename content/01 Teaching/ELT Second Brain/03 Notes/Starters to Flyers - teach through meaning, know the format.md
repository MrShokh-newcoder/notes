> The official Starters–Flyers handbook: familiarise children with the format 'whilst continuing their normal teaching programmes, and concentrate on teaching through a focus on meaning and context'. Test writers use only the published vocabulary and grammar lists.

## Recommendations
- Teach the published wordlists and grammar lists: the tests use only that language.
- Use pictures heavily: match words to pictures; draw from descriptions.
- Practise spelling and common letter patterns; clear, non-joined handwriting.
- Listen to whole dialogues before answering; include British and American accents.
- Drill confusable words and L1 false friends.
- Stories, word games and puzzles for vocabulary.
- Time limits on some tasks to build pacing.
- Reduce test anxiety, e.g. explain the format in the child's L1.
- Classroom instructions and social phrases in English.

## Limits
Promotional; no studies cited. Date unclear (file name 2024, internal copyright 2018). Only the first part (to Flyers Reading) was read in full.

## Apply in my context *(Claude)*
- "Explain the format in L1" is official Cambridge advice. It's useful backing for planned L1 use with parents and managers who expect "English only".
- Map Kid's Box units against the wordlists once, and flag off-list items as receptive only.
