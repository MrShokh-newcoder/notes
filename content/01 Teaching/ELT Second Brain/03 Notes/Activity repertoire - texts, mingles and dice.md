> Three low-prep repertoires: go beyond comprehension with a text, run mingles well, and add randomness to drills with a dice.

## Ten things to do with a text (Humphreys)
Critique the message · read aloud or retell with partners supplying missing words · redraw and describe · write from the title only, then partners guess it · remove a paragraph for students to write · upgrade key phrases (synonyms, fixed expressions) · act or mime with fading prompts · write exam questions on it.

## Mastering the mingle ([Houston](https://ihteachenglish.com/resources/mastering-the-mingle/))
Before: prepare the room and rules. During: join in. After: summarise the language. Variants: music; two facing lines with one-minute swaps; half the class moves; team races. Uses: Find someone who…, names in week one, guess the question, compliments.

## Dice ([Zapart](https://ihteachenglish.com/resources/101-ways-to-use-a-dice/))
The roll picks an animal to mime ("You are a big angry cat"), a true/false sentence, a linking word to finish a sentence starter, "There is / There are", or a question word for group discussion.
