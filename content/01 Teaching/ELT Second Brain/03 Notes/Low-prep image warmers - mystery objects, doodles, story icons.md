> Three warmers that need one slide each: speculate about a mystery object then check, describe students' doodles in their own coursebooks, and build a story from a set of icons.

## The three warmers
- **Mystery object** ([post](https://theteacherjames.com/2021/04/05/almost-infinite-elt-ideas-mystery-objects/)): invent a backstory, then research what it really is; or write the object's biography (who used it, where it came from).
- **Coursebook doodles** ([post](https://theteacherjames.com/2021/03/29/coursebook-creativity-warmer/)): show photos of doodles, elicit *doodle*, describe; students doodle on a page of their own book and describe it to a partner.
- **Story icons** ([post](https://theteacherjames.com/2021/05/28/storytelling-icons-warmer/)): a slide of icons (alien, crime, spooky sets); short prep time; pairs tell the story.

## Limits
No aims, levels or feedback stage. Warmers, not lessons.

## Make them earn their time *(Claude)*
Add a language target: mystery objects → modals of deduction (*It might be… It can't be…*, PET Speaking Part 3/4); story icons → narrative tenses and sequencers; then a 2-minute feedback stage with one upgraded sentence per pair.
