> 'Let's talk' isn't a method. Speaking grows from comprehensible oral input, retrieval, automatisation of high-frequency chunks, explicitly trained interaction and fluency work, sequenced from controlled to spontaneous with low anxiety.

## Ten principles
1. Flood with comprehensible oral input first. 2. Retrieval over exposure. 3. Recycle orally. 4. Automate high-frequency language. 5. Chunks before rules. 6. Teach speaking as micro-skills. 7. Controlled → spontaneous. 8. Reduce cognitive load. 9. Train interaction explicitly. 10. Low-anxiety environment.

## Sequence
Comprehensible input → retrieval → guided practice → structured interaction → semi-spontaneous → spontaneous. Fluency tasks: 4/3/2, Market Place, Messengers.

## Before a role-play
Read key sentences aloud → pair oral retrieval (faulty echo, delayed repetition, oral ping-pong) → correct planted errors → sentence-puzzle race.
