> Annie Thomson (IATEFL 2026): on 7–8-week intensives, the PDA's reflection and action stages got rushed. Six timetable changes fixed most of it. Time is the binding constraint.

## Six changes
1. Hold the PDA input session *after* the diagnostic lessons.
2. Session time on **action vs data collection**; SMART actions.
3. Colleagues as data collectors and models; peer observation and plan feedback.
4. A curated reading set for the key stage.
5. Deliberately planned developmental (observed, unassessed) lessons with a clear feedback focus.
6. Timetabled in-class writing time for the reflection and action write-ups (30–45 min, with reflection prompts).

## What trainees valued
Peer feedback and colleague observation helped most. Smaller, actionable focus areas worked better. Data-tool design and analysis still needed support.

## Caveats
One tutor's context; small follow-up data (2 of 5 trainees did further action research). Conference notes.

## Apply in my context *(Claude)*
- If you take DELTA M2 part-time while teaching at Rahimov School, the bottleneck shifts from time to *observers*. Arrange a peer-observation partner from the Akademik Rivojlanish group before the course starts.
