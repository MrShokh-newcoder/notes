> In a fully online flipped programme (>20,000 students, two Peruvian universities), teachers valued the extra speaking time. The main problem was uneven pre-class preparation, handled with recaps, modelling and scaffolding rather than penalties.

## Findings (teachers' perspectives)
- Learner autonomy has to be **taught**, not assumed.
- Technology enables the model; teacher mediation makes it work.
- Anxiety about mistakes remains a speaking barrier online.

## Moves
Short recap of pre-class content; re-model tasks before speaking; teach study planning; live time for speaking and feedback.

## Limits
The publisher's own impact study of its own platform (Evolve Digital); teacher sample size not reported; fully online university context.

## Apply in my context *(Claude)*
- For a homeschool or LMS product: don't assume pre-class completion. Build the recap into the live session and track completion as data, not as a discipline issue.
