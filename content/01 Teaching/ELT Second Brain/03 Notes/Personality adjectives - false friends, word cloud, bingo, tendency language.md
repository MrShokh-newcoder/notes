> A B2 vocabulary review that moves from recall (a class word cloud) to discrimination (false friends such as sensitive/sensible) to personalised speaking and writing with modifiers and tendency language.

## Sequence (B2)
1. Books open to review the unit's adjectives, then books closed: students send all they remember via Wooclap → word cloud (frequency = size).
2. AI-made slides explain false-friend pairs (*sensitive/sensible*, *considerate/thoughtful*) with MC and gap-fill items.
3. **Personality bingo:** 6-square grid, 4–5 positive + 1–2 negative adjectives; teacher gives definitions or opposites.
4. Speaking: justify choices with modifiers (*fairly, quite, rather, pretty, extremely*).
5. Homework: 100–120-word email replying to a flat-share ad, two positive traits and one negative softened with *I tend to… / I have a tendency to…*.

## Why it's good *(Claude)*
Recall before recognition (the word cloud is retrieval practice), then discrimination of confusable pairs, then generative use in a personal context. That's the ordering vocabulary research supports. The homework is a ready PET/FCE email task.
- Check AI explanations before class; false-friend explanations are where models get confidently wrong.
