> A curated, maintained map of who has written what on each DELTA module. Use it as a directory, not an endorsed list.

## Highest-value pointers
- **Module 1**: ELT Concourse free Module One course (her top pick); Damian Williams, *How to Pass Delta* (e-book).
- **Module 2**: Lizzie Pinard's "Delta tips" series (background essays, lesson plans). Lizzie Pinard is also in your bookmarks: [[Teaching Bookmarks Index]].
- **Module 3**: Lina Gordyshevskaya's "So you wanna do Delta Module 3"; Jim Fuller (Sponge ELT) guide and reading list; Pinard's section-by-section guides.
- Get a tutor or reader for drafts (Martin Hajek).
- Don't copy assignments. Cambridge treats plagiarism seriously.

## Caveats
Last updated March 2024 (by Phil Longwell). Many links are 2013–2021, liveness unchecked, some based on the old exam.
