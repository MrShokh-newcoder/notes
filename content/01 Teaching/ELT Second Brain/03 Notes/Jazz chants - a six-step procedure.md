> Carolyn Graham's jazz chants keep the natural stress and intonation of speech (unlike poems or raps), often as dialogue. They build pronunciation, fluency, chunks, grammar and confidence.

## Procedure (from the post)
1. Set the context; pre-teach vocabulary.
2. One or two pre-questions (*What does Davina like?*).
3. A first listening that shows understanding.
4. Chant and act it out with puppets, flashcards or actions.
5. Split the class to ask and answer; swap; keep rhythm (count in to four).
6. Pairs/groups practise, then perform; older children write parallel versions.
- Rounds work better with older children.
