> Most coursebook grammar and vocabulary exercises have one right answer. Remove the constraint (the verb in brackets, the word bank, one column of a matching task) so learners produce their own answers: more practice, more thinking, often more interesting results.

## Conversions (Ur Tip 9, from my notes)
- *She ... (leave) at six* → ignore the bracket: any past verb (*came, spoke, woke up*), or keep *left* and let learners finish the sentence (*left London, left her husband*).
- Gap-fill with a word bank → remove the bank; learners use context.
- Matching → delete one column; learners invent the matches.
- Teachers expect open versions to be too hard; usually they aren't.

## Why it works *(Claude)*
More correct answers = more retrieval and more generative use of the target form (deeper processing). It also gives every level something to do: a built-in differentiation device.
