> Repetition is essential, but unchanged repetition loses learners. 'The challenge isn't repetition itself – it's how we design it.' Change one or two parameters per round: partner, time limit, input, mode.

## Procedure
1. Core task (e.g. describe an image to a partner).
2. Each round, change one element: new partner · time limit · new image · spoken → written · add a constraint.
3. Optional: her *Task Tweaker* tool generates six variations from the task, objective, level and constraints ("low-resource setting").

## Limits
No research cited. "It won't do anything a skilled teacher couldn't do."

## Theory behind it *(Claude)*
This is **task repetition with variation**: Bygate (2001) found gains in fluency and complexity on repeated tasks, and Nation's 4/3/2 uses a changing partner and shrinking time. It also implements "spacing in varied contexts" ([[Spacing and interleaving beat massed practice]]).

## Apply in my context *(Claude)*
- Prepare 2e speaking tasks can be run 3× across a unit with one parameter changed each time. It's a cheap way to satisfy the repetition principle without boredom.
