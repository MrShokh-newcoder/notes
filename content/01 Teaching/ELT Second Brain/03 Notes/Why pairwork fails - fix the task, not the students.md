> 'Your students aren't lazy. The task gave them nothing to say.' Pairwork fails for four fixable reasons: no information gap, unclear instructions, poor pairing, no finish line.

## Checklist
1. Build a **gap** (different information or pictures, or a decision): "Find two things you both did and one thing only your partner did."
2. **Demo** the first exchange with a strong student.
3. 2 **ICQs**.
4. **Time + output**: "Three minutes. Agree on one."
5. **Monitor** without sitting down.
6. **Board** 2–3 good items and 2–3 errors afterwards.
- Change pairs at least once a lesson: similar levels for fluency, mixed levels for support.
- Extension tasks for early finishers to prevent L1 drift.

## Apply in my context *(Claude)*
- "L1 drift" in Tashkent classes usually means a missing gap or finish line, not defiance. Audit Prepare speaking tasks against points 1 and 4 before blaming the class.
