> A real candidate's lexis LSA essay: non-transparent multiword verbs at B1, combining a text-based approach (for meaning and collocation) with guided discovery (for syntax).

> [!note] A candidate's essay, not a Cambridge model. 2015 criteria.

## Analysis covered
Types: prepositional / phrasal / phrasal-prepositional. Syntax: transitivity and separability. Semantics: transparency, polysemy. Register, collocation, phonology: prepositional verbs stress the **verb**, phrasal verbs stress the **particle**.

## Teaching solutions (each with a rationale)
- Text-based presentation (meaning in context; collocation).
- Guided discovery: give examples, learners sort the rest by transitivity and separability.
- Gapped concordance lines for collocation and polysemy.
- Backchaining or choral drilling for stress.
- Select items by frequency, text type and learner needs.
