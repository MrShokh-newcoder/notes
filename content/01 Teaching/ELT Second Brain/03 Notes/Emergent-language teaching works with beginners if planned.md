> "But beginners…" is often a deficit excuse for avoiding participatory approaches. Very low-level adults can communicate well if the teacher keeps adult expectations and plans the structure that lets language emerge.

## Worked example (Entry 1 / A1 adult ESOL)
1. Picture of a house → elicit *house* → "What is in your house?"; rooms and furniture emerge.
2. Follow up a learner's answer (*I like kitchen*) with *Why?*, building to *I like cooking in kitchen*.
3. Turn it into a frame: *I like ___ in my ___*; use for language-experience writing.
4. Allow L1 in pairs or asides; no English-only rule.
5. Plan around communication goals; drop slides and workbooks where not needed.

## Limits
Experience-based; one example; no time-cost discussion.

## Apply in my context *(Claude)*
- Starters/Movers: the same picture → question → frame → personal sentence chain is a perfect bridge from chants to writing.
