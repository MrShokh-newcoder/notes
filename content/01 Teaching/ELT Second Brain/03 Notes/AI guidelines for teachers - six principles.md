> Six principles for AI in ELT: human first, privacy and data rights, ethics and bias, safety, transparency, accountability. Each comes with concrete actions such as removing personal data and disclosing AI use to learners.

## Actions under the principles
- Check AI output for accuracy and inclusion before learners see it.
- Remove personal data before using AI with learner work.
- Tell learners how AI is used and get permission.
- When citing AI output, name the tool and version.
- Keep human oversight over any AI-assisted marking.
- (Unsourced claim on the page: AI queries use ~5–10× the energy of a web search.)

## Apply in my context *(Claude)*
- Directly relevant to RS Insight, Coursebook-to-Lesson and the LMS. A one-paragraph AI-use statement for parents and teachers, based on these six principles, would pre-empt trust problems.
- Anonymise student scores before analysis in any AI tool.
