> A child with mild hearing loss seemed to join in the class chorus but had mapped 'sandwich' to a /k/ onset (and similar errors with avocado and helicopter). Apparent choral participation can conceal that a learner never heard the word correctly.

## Takeaway
- Choral repetition shows that the group is producing sound, not that each child perceived it.
- Check individuals: quick "say it to your partner" rounds, picture-pointing, or the teacher circulating during choral work.

## Limits
A single anecdote, shown in an embedded video; a commenter links it to L1 synonym retrieval, unreferenced.

## Apply in my context *(Claude)*
- For Starters/Movers groups: follow every choral drill with an individual or pair round, and keep an eye out for children who mouth along. Undiagnosed hearing issues are not rare in primary.
