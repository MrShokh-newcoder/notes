> PD is usually judged by implementation or competency gains (e.g. Choi & Kang 2019). What lasts is often a mindset, shaped by unplanned and collegial learning. Design PD that gives teachers agency, then back the initiatives they start.

## Questions to ask
- What exactly should stick: the programme, its practices, or a way of thinking?
- Do we count informal, collegial, self-chosen learning?
- What outlived the last "passion project"? (Her case: the RP Tokyo reflective group, 2016–19.)
- "Sustainability to me means my own growth and that of my community."

## Source
Choi & Kang (2019) "Sustainability of cooperative professional development: focused on teachers' efficacy", *Sustainability* 11(3), 585. Fanselow's "Believe nothing I say".

## Apply in my context *(Claude)*
- Give the Kasbiy rivojlanish checklisti an **agency** criterion (the teacher chose and led something) alongside **implementation** criteria. Otherwise it measures compliance (cf. [[Beyond discipline - compliance is not commitment]], applied to teachers).
