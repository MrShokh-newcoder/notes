> Showing A1+/A2 learners a waveform of speech makes stress visible: the tallest peak is the stressed syllable or word. It helped with a coursebook listening and gave students confidence with polite intonation.

## Procedure (A1+/A2)
1. Elicit word stress in familiar words; record yourself; show the waveform; point to the tallest peak.
2. Load the coursebook audio; play it sentence by sentence; match the tallest peak to the stressed word.
3. Look at the melody (rise or fall); practise more naturally.
4. Record each student saying one sentence; check the stress placement.
5. Role-play adapted from the dialogue, focusing on polite intonation and stress.
Tools: online voice recorder, mp3cut; Audacity or Praat for accuracy.

## Limits
One lesson; "tallest = stressed" is a simplification (pitch and duration matter too); check data rules before uploading student voices.

## Apply in my context *(Claude)*
- On the smartboard, Audacity's waveform is visible to the whole class. It's an engaging way to show stress-timing to Uzbek-L1 learners, whose rhythm tends to be more even.
