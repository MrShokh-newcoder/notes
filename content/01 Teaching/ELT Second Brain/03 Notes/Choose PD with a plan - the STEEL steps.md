> Faced with a buffet of online courses, choose PD against long-term goals, context and time, and use it in five steps (STEEL) so it turns into classroom change.

## STEEL
1. **S**tudents' needs → your learning need: one classroom area, one SMART goal.
2. **T**heory and practice in balance: prefer courses with reliable research.
3. **E**xperiment in your own context, with time to try.
4. **E**valuate what worked; adapt.
5. **L**earn with others: a buddy or a community of practice.
- Selection questions include: *Does this contribute to my longer-term plan? Is my learning measurable?*

## Evidence cited
Richardson & Maggioli (2018) Cambridge Papers in ELT; Kennedy (2016) *Review of Educational Research* 86(4); Wiliam (2016, 2019).

## Limits
Advocacy; STEEL itself untested.

## Apply in my context *(Claude)*
- Run DELTA prep through it: Module 1 reading is "T", but the LSA cycles are "E–E–L". Pick one focus per term.
