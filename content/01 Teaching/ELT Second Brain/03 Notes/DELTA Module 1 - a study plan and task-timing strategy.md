> One candidate's Merit route (2018): a 3-month tutor-marked course, heavy reading with colour-coded notes, two rounds of past papers, terminology drilled on Quizlet, and a fixed task order and timing for each paper.

> [!warning] One candidate's strategy, 2018 exam
> Not Cambridge guidance. Check the current handbook. This is the third task numbering in the vault after Swift (2012) and Pinard (2013).

## Plan
- Distance Delta M1 course (~3 months, tutor-marked mock).
- Notes on every input reading (hers ran to 104 A4 pages).
- Past papers twice; examiner reports where still available.
- Quizlet: two sets of 300+ terms.
- Treat preparation as a project with deadlines.

## Her timings (2018 format)
- **Paper 1**: T1 5 min · T2 10 · T3 10 · T4 20 · T5 45. Order: T5 b–d → T4 → T3 → T2 → T5a → T1.
- **Paper 2**: T1 15 · T2 40 · T3 35. Order: T2 → T3 → T1.

## Reading list
Parrott, *Grammar for English Language Teachers*; Thornbury, *About Language* and *The New A–Z of ELT*; Richards & Rodgers, *Approaches and Methods* or Larsen-Freeman & Anderson, *Techniques & Principles*; Hedge, *Teaching and Learning in the Language Classroom*; Lightbown & Spada, *How Languages are Learned*.
