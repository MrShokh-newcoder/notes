> Complement the comprehension (testing) model with a process model that teaches learners to decode the sound stream. Field's eight priorities put word recognition, 'islands of reliability', first.

## Field's eight priorities (as Smith summarises them)
1. Make **process** central.
2. Focus on **decoding**.
3. **Word recognition** first: "islands of reliability".
4. Build meaning from general context more than co-text. "Co-text cannot [be] assumed to be available until a certain level of decoding ability has been achieved."
5. **Strategy instruction** early.
6. Integrate processes and strategies.
7. A **diagnostic** approach.
8. **Authentic speech** from early on, with level-appropriate tasks.

## Procedures
Transcription of short segments; intensive sound-to-spelling decoding before comprehension; strategy training (redundancy, hypothesising from context and cognates); gap-fill scaffolds where transcription fails. Smith's own sequence: **macro → micro → macro** (understand a little → decode everything → understand everything).

## Limits
Smith: transcription is "a bit dull" for adolescents, so use it in moderation. Communication is still "the go-to way", supplemented by micro-listening. Field's book is dated 2009 here and 2008 elsewhere: it's 2008 (CUP).
