**Who:** ELT writer, teacher and teacher educator specialising in children aged 3–12 (Madrid-based). Author of *500 Activities for the Primary Classroom* (Macmillan 2007) and coursebooks (*Footprints*, *Hello, Robby Rabbit*).

**What:** A finished 26-post A–Z of primary ELT (Jan 2010 – Aug 2011), distilled from ~25 years of practice. Every post is practical, with numbered lists.

**Stance:** Sociocultural (Vygotsky, Bruner) and whole-child. Affect, relationships and routines are *preconditions* for learning. Sceptical of learning styles and of movement for its own sake.

**Why it matters for me:** The best compact YL methodology source in my bookmarks. It maps directly onto CTYL content and Starters–Flyers classes.
