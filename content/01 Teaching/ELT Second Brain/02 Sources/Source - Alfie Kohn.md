**Who:** US writer and lecturer on education, parenting and motivation. Books include *Punished by Rewards* (1993; 25th-anniversary edition 2018), *No Contest*, *Beyond Discipline*, *The Homework Myth* (2006), *Unconditional Parenting* (2005), *The Schools Our Children Deserve* (1999), *The Myth of the Spoiled Child* (2014).

**Stance:** Rewards, praise, grades and punishment are all control ("doing to" rather than "working with"). They erode interest, risk-taking and the quality of thinking, and buy only temporary compliance.

**Why it matters for me:** The strongest critique in the vault of the reward systems I design. Read it *with* the counter-literature (Cameron & Pierce 1994; Deci, Koestner & Ryan 1999; Henderlong & Lepper 2002; Cooper et al. 2006), summarised in each note. Kohn is usually directionally right and overstated in degree.
