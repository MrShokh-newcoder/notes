**Who:** ELT teacher educator and methodology writer (NZ-born, based in Spain). Author of *An A–Z of ELT* (2006) / *The New A–Z of ELT* (2017), *About Language*, *Big Questions in ELT*; co-author of *Teaching Unplugged* (with Luke Meddings, 2009); series editor of Cambridge Handbooks for Language Teachers. The blog is independent: no publisher or institutional funding.

**What:** A numbered glossary (001–200), each entry a short essay on an ELT/SLA concept with references and long comment threads (Krashen, Jordan and others argue in them). Most posts are from 2010–2011.

**Stance:** Emergentist / usage-based (N. Ellis, Christiansen & Chater, Halliday), with a cognitivist line on attention and noticing. Sceptical of PPP and the grammar syllabus; sympathetic to Dogme and TBLT but candid about their practicality; argues regularly with Krashen's input-only position.

**Why it matters for me:** The single best terminology source for DELTA Module 1. Each entry is a compact, referenced definition plus the counter-arguments in the comments.
