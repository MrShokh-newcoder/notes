**Who:** PhD (Institute of Education, London); MA TEFL; 28 years at ESADE Barcelona; co-ran an MA TESOL; author of *Theory Construction in SLA* (2004), with Long of *English Language Teaching: Now and How It Could Be* (2022), and with Gray of "We need to talk about coursebooks" (*ELTJ* 2020). Teaches a TBLT course (SLB Cooperative).

**What:** "A critique of ELT teacher trainers": long polemics against coursebook-driven PPP and named figures (Ur, Harmer, Dellar, Anderson, Holliday, Kiczkowiak), arguing from instructed-SLA research.

**Read critically:** Often personal and aggressive, which commenters criticise. His strongest value is the SLA research he cites. The best counter-voices are in his comment threads (Thornbury, Gregg). He defends the NS/NNS distinction (citing Long 2007; Birdsong 1992) against critics of native-speakerism.
