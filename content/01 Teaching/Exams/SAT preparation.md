## 3-Month SAT Math Plan

I want to take SAT english & math. I have band 7,0 IELTS but I don't know so much about math. Give me 3month detailed plan to get prepared for digital SAT math section

Preparing for the SAT math section in three months can be a manageable goal. Here's a detailed plan to help you get ready:

##### Month 1: Foundations and Basics

**Week 1-2:**

Familiarize yourself with the SAT math format and question types.
Review basic arithmetic operations, fractions, decimals, and percentages.
Practice mental math skills.

**Week 3-4:**

Focus on algebraic concepts, including equations and inequalities.
Learn how to solve linear equations and work with algebraic expressions.
Practice solving basic algebra problems.

#### Month 2: Intermediate Skills

**Week 5-6:**

Dive into geometry. Study concepts like angles, triangles, and circles.
Work on your understanding of geometric formulas.
Practice solving geometry problems.

**Week 7-8:**

Move on to advanced algebra topics such as quadratic equations and functions.
Learn how to solve systems of linear equations.
Continue practicing algebraic skills.

##### Month 3: Advanced Practice and Test Strategy

**Week 9-10:**

Start practicing with official SAT math practice tests.
Identify your weak areas and focus on them.
Brush up on data analysis and statistics.

**Week 11-12:**

Continue taking full-length practice tests under timed conditions.
Review and refine your test-taking strategies.
Pay attention to pacing and time management.

#### **Throughout the 3 months:**

Use SAT prep books, online resources, and tutoring if necessary.
Join online forums or study groups for support and additional practice.
Keep a detailed study journal to track your progress and areas of improvement.
Don't forget to review your mistakes and learn from them.

By following this plan diligently, you should see significant improvement in your SAT math skills over three months. Remember that consistent practice and targeted studying are key to success on the SAT. Good luck with your preparation!

## Books & Resources 

Here are some of the best SAT math prep books and resources that you can use for your SAT math preparation:

**The College Board's Official SAT Study Guide**: This book contains real SAT questions from past exams. It's one of the most authentic resources for SAT preparation.

**Khan Academy**: Khan Academy offers free SAT math lessons and practice questions. Their content is aligned with the College Board's SAT test.

**Barron's SAT Math Workbook**: Barron's is known for its comprehensive study guides. This workbook provides in-depth practice and explanations for SAT math.

**The Princeton Review's Cracking the SAT Premium Edition**: This book offers strategies, practice tests, and review material, including a section dedicated to SAT math.

**Dr. Steve Warner's "28 SAT Math Lessons to Improve Your Score in One Month**": A focused book that provides targeted lessons to boost your SAT math score quickly.

**PWN the SAT**: Math Guide by Mike McClenathan: Highly regarded for its clear explanations and strategies to tackle SAT math problems.

**SAT Math Prep Course by Jeff Kolby:** Offers a structured approach to mastering SAT math concepts with practice problems and explanations.

**UWorld SAT Prep:** UWorld provides a comprehensive set of SAT math questions that mimic the real test. It's an excellent resource for targeted practice.

**PrepScholar SAT Online Prep:** PrepScholar offers an online program with personalized lessons and practice questions tailored to your needs.

**Varsity Tutors SAT Math Practice Tests:** This website offers a variety of free SAT math practice tests to help you assess your skills.

Remember to use a combination of these resources for a well-rounded preparation. Practice is key to success on the SAT math section, so make sure to dedicate time to solving practice questions and reviewing your mistakes. Good luck with your SAT math preparation!
