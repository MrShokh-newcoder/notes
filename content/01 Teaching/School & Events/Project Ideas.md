### RS: Debate Club
RS Debate Club is an event which will be conducted by high school students for intermediate , upper-intermediate and IELTS level students
It can be helpful to improve their critical thinking and speaking skills as well. There will be a number of hot topics to discuss!

### RS: Master Class 
RS Master Class is an excellent opportunity for students of Rahimov School to learn essential skills that are useful for their own personal and educational purposes! In the events there will be teachers, businessmen and other successful people who are eager to share their experiences and knowledge!

### RS: Quiz
RahimovSchool Quiz is a competition where students will be able to work as a team. There will be different types of quizzes that are challenging to do so that students will improve their overall performance, team work, including critical thinking, leadership skills... etc
