✨Ideas for teambuilding:

 - Scavenger Hunt: Divide participants into teams and create a list of items or clues to find within the picnic area.

 - Tug-of-War: Organize a classic tug-of-war tournament between teams.

 - Three-Legged Race: Pair up participants and have them compete in a three-legged race.

 - Werewolf/Mafia: In this social deduction game, players try to determine who among them is the werewolf/mafia. It involves deception, strategy, and teamwork. It's always a hit with big groups!

 - Charades: A classic party game that is perfect for large groups. Players act out specific words or phrases without speaking while their teammates try to guess what they're acting out.

 - Egg Drop Challenge: Provide materials (e.g., straws, tape, paper) and have teams design a contraption to protect an egg from breaking when dropped from a height.

 - Blindfolded Obstacle Course: One team member is blindfolded while the others guide them verbally through an obstacle course.

 - Watermelon Eating Contest: Compete to see who can eat a slice of watermelon the fastest, hands-free.

 - Water Relay: Teams compete in a relay race while carrying cups of water on their heads or with spoons.
