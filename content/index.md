## How the notes are built
Each idea note has: **Claim → Mechanism/theory → Classroom moves → Limits & critique → Apply in my context → Connections**.
- Anything marked *(Claude)* is added interpretation or outside knowledge, not the source's claim. Verify it before citing.
- Content is paraphrased; quotes are under 25 words. Always cite the original post or study, not this vault.
- New notes follow Template - Idea note.
