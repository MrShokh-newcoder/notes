> The needs-analysis section is judged on how explicitly the principles of needs analysis and testing (practicality, reliability, validity) shaped your tools, and on a traceable chain: tool → finding → course priority.

> [!warning] 2013 DELTA format
> Written as a candidate in 2013. Word counts, task numbers and handbook page references have changed since. Keep the method and check the details in the current Cambridge handbook.

## What to do
- Profile the group precisely: *these* learners, on whom the analysis was done.
- Justify each tool (questionnaire, interview, diagnostic test): why this one and not another.
- Before designing a tool, ask: *what do I need to find out, and how will the answer change the course?*
- Pilot the tools on a colleague.
- Cross-reference with short bracketed labels, e.g. (A1.vi) for appendix items.
- Evaluate each tool's pros and cons; argue it's the best compromise for your context.
