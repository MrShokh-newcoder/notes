> Millin's reading list for a Module 3 on a 20-hour IELTS reading and writing course: exam classes, testing, course design, reading methodology.

## Exam classes
- Burgess & Head, *How to Teach for Exams* (2005)
- P. May, *Exam Classes* (1996)
- Prodromou (1995) on backwash; Murphy (2000) on evaluation

## Testing and assessment
- H. D. Brown, *Language Assessment: Principles and Classroom Practices* (2003)
- Arthur Hughes, *Testing for Language Teachers* (2nd ed., 2003)
- Gronlund, *Assessment of Student Achievement*
- Harris & McCann, *Assessment* (1994)

## Course design
- Richards, *Curriculum Development in Language Teaching* (2001)
- Graves (ed.), *Teachers as Course Developers* (1996); Graves, *Designing Language Courses* (2000)
- Woodward, *Planning Lessons and Courses* (2001)

## Reading
- Christine Nuttall, *Teaching Reading Skills in a Foreign Language* (3rd ed.)

## Also
Harmer, *The Practice of English Language Teaching* (2007); Thornbury, *A–Z of ELT* (2006).

## Caveats
Dated: *Cambridge IELTS 3* (2002) and old band descriptors. Some titles are out of print.
