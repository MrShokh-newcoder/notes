> Planned CPD breaks down when life intervenes. Much valuable development is unplanned: a webinar, a conversation, a classroom experiment. 'What else have you done instead?' makes it visible. Development is a patchwork.

## Formats
- **Pop-up PD**: ~1 hour, topics suggested by teachers, round-table.
- **Lesson Jam**: peer feedback on lesson ideas.
- **Just-in-time PD**: learning a skill at the point of need.
- A "PD patchwork" meeting ([2026](https://wednesdayseminars.wordpress.com/2026/09/01/professional-development-as-patchwork-piece/)): each member maps their informal learning (one member's included DELTA Module 1). "Reflection/Reflective Practice is a skill (we may take it for granted)."
