> Repeat the same song through different modes (movement, pictures, reading, pairs) rather than identically: 'varied repetition'. The post frames this with Gardner's multiple intelligences via Armstrong's MI Pizza.

## Activities
- Alphabet song with body letter shapes (a whole-body X).
- Colour-word songs with pictures and movement.
- Prepositions song acted as a scene (students become a snake and a rock).
- Keep songs short and tied to one structure; switch to individual or written work after.

## Read critically *(Claude)*
- The cited Gardner (2013) article is titled "'Multiple intelligences' are not 'learning styles'": matching activities to children's intelligences isn't supported. The *variation* is what helps: it gives repetition without satiation and multiple retrieval cues (encoding variability).
- Promotional (*Everybody Up*).
