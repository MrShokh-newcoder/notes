> Subject teachers send 10–12 key words two weeks ahead. The EAL team builds a reference sheet with context, definitions, visuals, collocations and 'questions your teacher might ask', and learners get a head start before the topic.

## Cycle
1. Subject teacher → 10–12 key items, ~2 weeks before the topic.
2. EAL team → keyword sheet: words in context, definitions, visuals, collocations, matching, "questions your teacher might ask (using this vocab)".
3. A week ahead → shared via Google Classroom; learners can translate into their home language.
4. Wordwall check tasks with due dates; the data shows which items are hard.
5. Review after the topic, before assessment. Repeat.

## Limits
Subject-teacher buy-in; the impact is "more a feeling than it is tangible". Command words and Tier 2 language (Zwiers: "mortar around the bricks") may matter more than subject nouns.
