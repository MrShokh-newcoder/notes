> Metaphors shape how we think about AI ('intelligence' is itself one). Applying Mason's (2018) categories to a Pearson post, Kerr finds 'tool', 'integration' and benevolent-agent verbs ('offers', 'enhances') that reinforce the brand. Alternatives: cultural technology, stochastic parrot, autocomplete on steroids.

## Sources
Lakoff & Johnson (1980); Mason (2018); Mitchell (2024) *Science*; Weller (2022) *Metaphors of Ed Tech*; Thornbury (1991) "Metaphors we work by", *ELT Journal* 45/3; Cogo, Patsko & Szoke (2024) *ELT Journal* 78/4.

## Use
- Analyse AI ads and conference abstracts with colleagues using these frames.
- For my products: "tutor"/"coach"/"companion" set expectations; choose framing that keeps learner agency and shows limits. Audit UI copy for "enhances/empowers".
