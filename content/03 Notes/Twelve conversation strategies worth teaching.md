> Coursebook strategy boxes are thin. Listen for the moments when strategies are needed and teach them as they arise: backchannelling, follow-up questions, fillers, repair and clarification, vague language, hedging.

## Highlights
- **Backchannelling** ("yeah", "really?") plus follow-up Wh-questions, taught together.
- **QAAA** for discussions: A asks a question; B answers, adds a point, asks back.
- **Fillers** ("you know", "I mean"): raise awareness; let your own speech model them.
- **Repair**: "Sorry, what was that?" / "Do you mean…?" instead of a bare "What?"
- **Anecdote retelling**: retell several times, adding one strategy each time.
- "There may be loads of opportunities to upgrade learners' conversation skills, you've just got to know what to listen for."
