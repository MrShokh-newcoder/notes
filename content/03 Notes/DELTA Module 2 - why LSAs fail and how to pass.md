> From Millin's own LSA grades and tutor feedback: lessons fail on unrealistic timing, dependence on learner output in a weak context, poor set-up and unpredicted problems. Essays fail on signposting, a narrow source range and description without critique.

## Her LSAs
LSA1 grammar (conditionals) · LSA2 listening (transactional) · LSA3 writing (discursive essays) · LSA4 lexis (multi-part verbs).

## Lesson failure points
- Unrealistic timing.
- Relying on learner-generated output in a weak context.
- Weak task set-up; unpredicted problems. Trial activities on another class first.

## Background essay fixes
- Signpost with headings.
- A wider range of sources.
- Critical commentary, not description.
- Make activity descriptions reproducible.

## Experimental practice and PDA
- Include scanned learner output (her regret).
- The PDA's reflection and action stages are "by far the most interesting and useful part of the whole course".
- Module 3: use every draft and comment opportunity.

## Caveats
2013–14 rules; centres now set their own LSA areas. Personal opinion, not Cambridge-sanctioned.
