> Four candidates' routes (Charlie: 1-3-2 while a full-time DoS; Zhiwen: 3-1-2 by distance from China, then intensive M2; Sarah: 1-2-3 part-time with Merits; Ha Truong: M3 Merit in 8–9 months). Common lessons: plan early, choose an M3 specialism you know, get extra feedback, don't rush.

## Advice that recurs
- **Plan every assignment early**, especially working full-time (Charlie crammed and regretted it).
- **M2 final LSA:** pick a skill where progress is visible in 60 minutes (speaking, writing), not reading (Charlie).
- **M3 specialism:** one you know well, not one you're curious about (Zhiwen; Ha Truong).
- **Read the official reading list early** and relate it to your teaching (Zhiwen); intensive M2 leaves time only to skim.
- **Order:** 1→2→3 builds jargon and report writing (Sarah); theory first is also common (Charlie, 1-3-2).
- **Post-LSA group feedback** is where "real" learning happens; compare Merit vs Pass lessons (Sarah).
- **Proofreader** for assignments: if they can't follow it, the assessor may not (Sarah).
- **Principal Examiner's Reports** for M3 expectations; extra feedback from M3 graduates; submit appendices with drafts; halve teaching load in the final month (Ha Truong).
- "If you have the option to take things slowly with DELTA, take it." (Ha Truong)
