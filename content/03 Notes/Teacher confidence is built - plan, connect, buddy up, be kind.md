> Teacher confidence is context-dependent and grows with deliberate practice. Four levers: plan and anticipate, build relationships with learners, observe and get feedback from colleagues, and treat failures as feedback.

## The four tips
1. **Plan and prepare:** script task set-ups and instructions, check answer keys, use routines (think-pair-share), anticipate problems.
2. **Connect:** learn interests early; personalise topics; show real interest in answers.
3. **Buddy up:** observe experienced colleagues; ask for feedback on strengths; a community of practice.
4. **Be kind to yourself:** positive self-talk; failures as feedback; build on strengths.

## Evidence cited
Toropova et al. (2020); Wiens & Ruday (2014). Self-efficacy discussed without a source *(Bandura 1997 is the origin, Claude)*.
