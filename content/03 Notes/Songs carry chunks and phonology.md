> Songs make language memorable, repeatable and inclusive. They carry chunks and connected-speech features (linking, contractions, stress), and children not yet ready to sing can still join in with actions.

## Sequence (young children)
1. Teach the words (one verse per group, or elicit phrases from pictures).
2. Children invent actions and teach them to each other.
3. Chant the words with actions, adding a verse each time.
4. Move to the music; no singing yet.
5. Sing and act; children write new verses with actions and perform them.
- A welcome song marks the start of English time; songs can time routines like tidying up.

## Limits
Memory and special-needs claims (SEN, ADHD, dyslexia) are asserted without sources; no age range given.
