> One striking new word ('algorithmocracy': government by algorithm) anchors a sequence: lead-in about apps → pros and cons → research the 'filter bubble' → read and check → read and react → collocation gap-fill.

## Sequence (teens and adults)
1. Lead-in: which apps do you use, and how do they decide what you see?
2. Introduce the word and its definition.
3. Pros and cons of algorithmic decisions.
4. Web research: "What is the filter bubble? How can you break it?" Explain it to a lower-level peer.
5. Read and check predictions ("I was (partly) right about…").
6. Read and react ("I had no idea that…", "What I would add is…").
7. Collocation and phrasal-verb gap-fill (*pop up*, *filter out*, *amplify inequalities*) + match the halves.

## Limits
No retrieval step after the gap-fill; sources unvetted; no level or time guidance.
