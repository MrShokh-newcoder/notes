> An inner circle holds a seminar discussion while an outer circle observes with a simplified, single-criterion checklist (interaction only) and ticks set phrases they hear. Narrowing the focus made peer feedback usable for students new to giving it.

## Procedure (EAP, intermediate–upper-intermediate, ~50 min)
1. Groups: some discuss (inner), others observe (outer).
2. Observers get the course criteria cut down to the **interaction** column, with emoticons, plus a checklist of turn-taking and interrupting phrases to tick.
3. Swap roles.
4. Pair feedback: 5 minutes each (what went well, what to improve).
5. Collect reactions afterwards (comment thread).

## Limits
Some students felt shy or embarrassed; single EAP class.
