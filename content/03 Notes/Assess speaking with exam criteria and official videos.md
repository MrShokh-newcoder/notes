> Know the examiners' criteria, calibrate by scoring official speaking test videos against Bands 1/3/5, teach specific subskills, and give balanced, specific, actionable feedback. (B2 First upwards.)

## Strategies
1. Learn the criteria (at C1/C2, grammar and vocabulary split into Grammatical and Lexical Resource).
2. Calibrate: score sample videos, starting with Global Achievement.
3. Teach subskills: a "schwa hunt" (pronunciation); polite-disagreement role-play (interactive communication); 30-second mind-map talks (lexis).
4. Feedback: balanced, specific, actionable.

## Limits
- Publisher blog: practical and partly promotional; no research cited. Verify criteria wording against the official teacher guides and handbooks.
- The criteria grouping differs from the A2/B1 post. Check the official guide.
