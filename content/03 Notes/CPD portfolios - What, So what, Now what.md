> In the NILE / British Council / Uzbek Ministry 'CPD in State Schools in Uzbekistan' project, 800+ teachers in 116 schools kept a simple three-prompt portfolio. 'The portfolios helped us to see that we are already doing CPD.'

## The portfolio
1. **What?** What happened (the experience or action).
2. **So what?** What it means, and its implications.
3. **Now what?** One concrete thing to try next (weekly).
- Teachers choose their own evidence: notes, articles, observation forms, or just a few lines.

## What made it work
- A **school-based champion** teacher, plus a **cluster mentor** (national consultant).
- Collegial sharing: notice boards, small-group meetings.
- Counting emergent learning (a 5-minute colleague chat) as CPD.
- "Now what?" gives overloaded teachers a sense of control.

## Limits
Sponsored post (NILE); self-reported; no outcome data. "Not the solution to all teacher development challenges."
