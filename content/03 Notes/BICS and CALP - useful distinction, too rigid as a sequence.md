> Cummins distinguished conversational fluency (BICS, ~1–3 years) from academic language proficiency (CALP, ~5–7 years). Halbach (2012) criticises the sequential framing, the notion of 'context-reduced' language and the vague 'cognitive'. Use the distinction, not the timeline.

## Concepts
- **BICS / CALP** (Cummins, early 1980s; Cummins 2008 on its status).
- **Cummins Quadrant**: context-embedded ↔ context-reduced × cognitively undemanding ↔ demanding. Sequence units from embedded and easy toward reduced and demanding.
- **Common underlying proficiency** (the iceberg): L1 academic skills transfer.

## Critique
Halbach (2012) *Applied Linguistics* 33(4): the implied sequence may not hold; "context-reduced" is doubtful; "cognitive" is vague. Academic underachievement may come from lacking academic language and background knowledge.

## Practice
Visuals and concrete tasks first; allow L1; build background knowledge before academic texts; use an integrated scale (WIDA) rather than a rigid BICS/CALP split.
