> Kohn claims 'no evidence of any academic benefit' for homework in elementary or middle school and a weak link in high school. Change the default to 'no homework except when truly necessary'.

## Proposals (to principals)
- Educate staff on the research; drop minutes-per-night policies.
- Reduce the amount; check how long tasks really take.
- Only set work teachers would design themselves: "better to give no homework to anyone than the same homework to everyone".
- Involve students in decisions; try a homework-free week and measure.

## Counter-evidence *(Claude)*
Cooper, Robinson & Patall (2006, *Review of Educational Research*): positive homework–achievement associations, strongest at secondary and weak in primary. So Kohn is roughly right for primary and overstated for teens.
