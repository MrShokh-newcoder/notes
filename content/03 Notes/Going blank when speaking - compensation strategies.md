> Going blank in an L2 presentation is often driven by a default belief ("my English is bad so I'll forget"). Reflect on how you cope in your L1, then rehearse concrete fallbacks.

## Six strategies
1. Keywords on cards within reach.
2. Paraphrase with an example.
3. A mind map of main ideas to glance at.
4. Ask the audience for the word.
5. Admit it and pause.
6. Anticipate questions and prepare answers.
- Pick one or two, use them in the next presentation, record what happened.

## Theory *(Claude)*
These are Dörnyei & Scott's **communication (compensation) strategies**: circumlocution, appeal for help, use of fillers and pause. Teaching them explicitly is supported (Dörnyei 1995).
