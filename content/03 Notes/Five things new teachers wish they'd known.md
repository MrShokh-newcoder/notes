> Laura Patsko (IATEFL 2026): teaching isn't only about language; intelligibility means familiarity, not a neutral accent; pronunciation belongs in every lesson; solve for one, extend to many; caring has a cost.

## The five
1. Language teaching is not only about language.
2. Accent myths: intelligibility is largely **familiarity**, not a "neutral" accent. Ask learners what they want to sound like.
3. Pronunciation in every lesson: a "sound hunt" after reading; "odd one out" for stress.
4. Inclusion: "solve for one, extend to many" (Kat Holmes, *Mismatch*). Offer choice: written or spoken feedback; a blank page, gapped text or multiple choice for dictation.
5. Burnout: check in on colleagues ("are you ok?").
