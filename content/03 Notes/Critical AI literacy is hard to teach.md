> Tool know-how dates fast. The real target is critical and ethical AI literacy, which is hard to teach: critical thinking is domain-specific and needs dispositions, the evidence base is thin, and fully ethical use may be impossible. Language learning itself is the most workable domain.

## Five difficulties
1. Critical thinking is domain-specific (Willingham 2007).
2. It needs dispositions, not just skills (Facione 2000).
3. Media literacy alone doesn't solve misinformation (Bulger & Davidson 2018).
4. Little evidence about what works.
5. Ethical use is complicated by copyright and environmental costs.

## What can work
"Try, then reflect" tasks: learners test a tool against their own learning goals and judge its contribution (Pegrum, Hockly & Dudeney 2022 *Digital Literacies*). Build this longitudinally, not as a one-off. Scaffold self-regulation: AI's potential "is only likely to be realised if the learner is autonomous and self-regulating".
