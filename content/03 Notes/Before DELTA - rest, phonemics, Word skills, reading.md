> Eight things to do before Day 1: rest, read, choose centre and mode, sharpen Word, learn phonemics, choose LSA areas, choose a Module 3 specialism, build a network.

## Checklist
1. **Rest**: take a real holiday first (her main regret).
2. **Read** a few core books (below).
3. **Centre and mode**: ask past candidates (DELTA & DipTESOL Candidates & Survivors Facebook group).
4. **Word skills**: heading styles, auto table of contents, track changes, section breaks (portrait/landscape), repeating table header rows, Unicode font for IPA.
5. **Phonemics**: needed in Module 1 and throughout. Underhill's *Sound Foundations* and his pronunciation video.
6. **LSA areas**: check your centre's rules first.
7. **Module 3 specialism**: pick early.
8. **PLN** of fellow candidates.

## Books named
- Tricia Hedge, *Teaching and Learning in the Language Classroom*
- Michael Lewis, *The English Verb*
- Adrian Underhill, *Sound Foundations*
- Scott Thornbury, *About Language* (2nd ed.) and *The New A–Z of ELT*
- John Field, *Listening in the Language Classroom*
- Sally Burgess & Katie Head, *How to Teach for Exams*
- Jack C. Richards, *Curriculum Development in Language Teaching*
- David Nunan, *Syllabus Design* (she found it confusing)
- Macalister & Nation, *Language Curriculum Design* (2024 recommendation)
