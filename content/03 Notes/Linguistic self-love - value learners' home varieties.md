> Interview with Dr Kisha Bryan-Jordan (Tennessee State University). Learners should keep and value their home and community varieties while adding new forms; teachers need 'courageous care': examining their own beliefs about what sounds professional, academic or correct.

## Key ideas
- **Linguistic self-love:** learner varieties are resources, not things to "fix".
- **Courageous care:** audit your own judgements of "proper" English, then act on the audit.
- Advice for new teachers: know your *why*, find where you fit, protect your mental health.

## Limits
Advocacy interview; no classroom procedure, no research cited.
