> Jordan's positive proposal: Long's version of TBLT, built from a needs analysis of target tasks, with pedagogic tasks, 'modified, elaborated texts' and reactive focus on form. It's now offered in 'lighter, more feasible versions' for small schools.

## Course structure (SLB Cooperative, 100 hours)
1. Presenting TBLT
2. Designing a needs analysis (target tasks)
3. A task-based pedagogic unit
4. Task-based materials (modified, elaborated input)
5. Facilitating and evaluating tasks

Key text: Long (2015) *Second Language Acquisition and Task-Based Language Teaching*. Journal: *TASK* (2021–). Benjamins TBLT series.

## Long vs Ellis vs Willis *(Claude)*
- **Long**: strong TBLT; the syllabus *is* tasks from a needs analysis; focus on form is reactive only.
- **R. Ellis**: task-*supported* language teaching is legitimate; focused tasks are allowed.
- **Willis**: pre-task → task cycle → language focus, inside a general course.

## Limits
It's an advert for a paid course run by the author. It says nothing on exam-mandated contexts (Cambridge, IELTS) *(Claude)*.
