> By 2016, adaptive learning had fallen into Gartner's 'trough of disillusionment'. The Gates-funded ALMAP evaluation found no firm evidence of better grades or completion. Computer-adaptive *testing* is well validated; adaptive *courseware* is not.

## Evidence reviewed
- SRI/Gates ALMAP evaluation: no firm gains in grades or completion.
- Enyedy (2014) *Personalized Instruction: New Interest, Old Rhetoric, Limited Results* (NEPC).
- Murray & Pérez (2015), adaptive vs traditional learning.
- OECD (2015) *Students, Computers and Learning*.
- Industry reports (EdSurge "Decoding Adaptive", sponsored by Pearson) = marketing.
- "There is no magical silver bullet here." (Rahim Rajan, Gates Foundation)

## Implications for builders
- Separate adaptive testing (psychometrics, item response theory) from adaptive courseware in your claims.
- Ask for outcome data against controls, not engagement metrics.
- State exactly what adapts (item selection, feedback, pacing) and the evidence for each.
- Build local evaluation into pilots from day one.
