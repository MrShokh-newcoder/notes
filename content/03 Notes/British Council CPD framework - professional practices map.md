> A self-development map of professional practices (knowing the subject, understanding context and learners, professional identity, planning, managing, assessing, plus cross-cutting digital, inclusive, multilingual and 21st-century-skills practices), each with four levels: Awareness → Understanding → Engagement → Integration.

## Practices (as listed on the page)
- Knowing the subject
- Understanding my context · Understanding my learners · Understanding and developing my professional identity
- Planning lessons and courses · Managing the lesson · Assessing learning
- Cross-cutting: Using digital technologies · Using inclusive practices · Using multilingual practices · Using 21C / core skills

Levels: **Awareness, Understanding, Engagement, Integration**. Start anywhere. Full descriptors are in the [framework PDF](https://www.teachingenglish.org.uk/sites/teacheng/files/media/documents/CPD_framework_for_teachers.pdf), not yet processed.

## Limits
Aimed at public-sector schools. No research basis cited on the page. Earlier editions listed 12 practices with different labels *(Claude, verify)*.
