> Adaptive learning is pushed by commercial and philanthropic interests. Its data raises 'dataveillance' concerns, and dashboards can deskill teachers. It's a solution only if education is reduced to transmitting information, so define the problem first.

## Arguments
- Morozov: "Digital technologies might be a perfect solution to some problems, but those problems don't include education."
- Selwyn (2013) *Distrusting Educational Technology* / *Education in a Digital World*; Ball (2012) *Education Inc.*; Morozov (2013) *To Save Everything, Click Here*.
- Dashboards risk displacing teachers' own knowledge of students (deprofessionalisation).

## Design checklist (from the post's implications)
- What problem, for whom, and how will we know it's solved?
- Be transparent about data: what is captured, who sees it, why.
- Teacher override: dashboards inform decisions, they don't make them.
- Who profits from the student data?
