> Print vocabulary sets as cards for offline recycling. Use words plus gapped sentences rather than definitions, because co-text carries meaning. 20 items fit one page.

## Selected activities
1. **Match + recall**: match gapped-sentence cards to words, then recall the words with the word cards hidden.
2. **Peer assessment**: fill gaps on blank-backed cards, swap, check, score.
4. **How many do you remember?**: sort cards into known/unknown; repeat next lesson to see the gain.
6. **Guess the word**: explain the word without saying it or the bold chunk words.
7. **Put it in context**: write one sentence, then two (the first sets context, the second contains the target).
9. **Cloze on the board**: stick sentence strips under a word bank, to music.
10. **Closed-circle cloze**: find your group of three by matching strips.
