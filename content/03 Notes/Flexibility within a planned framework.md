> Flexibility is one of the five Fs (friendly, firm, fair, flexible, fun). The best lessons sometimes come from abandoning the plan, but flexibility mostly means building choices into a routine framework.

## Eleven ways to build in flexibility
Topics from children's interests · grouping criteria (friendship/ability/gender/random) · standing vs seated · repeat a popular game or song · timing matched to difficulty · who asks and answers · how answers are checked · teacher vs peer feedback · choice of task, order, method or homework · L1 to avoid misunderstanding or resolve conflict · reward systems "if used at all".

## Examples
Snow in Madrid; a shoebox of silkworms. "Here-and-now" relevance turned into lessons.

## Limits
Those moments "don't happen every day", so flexibility needs a repertoire and a routine base. "Flexible within a framework" comes from a commenter, endorsed by Read.
