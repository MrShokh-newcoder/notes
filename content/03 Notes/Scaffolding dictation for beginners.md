> Full dictation is one of the hardest beginner tasks: perception, segmentation, lexical access, prediction, spelling, handwriting and monitoring all at once. Scaffold it in a progression and keep learning-dictation separate from testing-dictation.

## The nine processes (a pedagogical model, not a literal account)
Acoustic input → phonological perception → segmentation → lexical access → grammatical/contextual prediction → orthographic retrieval → grapho-motor execution → working memory → monitoring.

## Progression (Language Gym Dictation Trainer)
1. Multiple choice (discrimination, no writing)
2. Missing detail (one element)
3. Sentence puzzle (reorder chunks)
4. Break the flow (mark word boundaries; segmentation)
5. Spot the word
6. Missing vowels / missing consonants
7. Guided dictation (support fades ~70% → 50% → 20% → 0)
8. Full dictation

Principles: familiar language, short sentences, strategic repetition, progressive difficulty, reflective questions after errors. "Don't repeatedly test the complex performance you eventually want."

## Limits & critique
- No empirical evidence cited for the sequence or the fade percentages.
