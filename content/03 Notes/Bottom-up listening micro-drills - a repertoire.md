> Listening is under-planned because it happens implicitly in oral work. Learners need lots of it, plus short drills on decoding sub-skills (phonemes, word boundaries, lexical retrieval, parsing) placed inside communicative work, not instead of it.

## Drill menu
- **Same or different?** minimal pairs.
- **Faulty echo / spot the error:** teacher reads with a deliberate learner-style mispronunciation.
- **Track the sound:** stand up when you hear the target sound in a song or rhyme (YL).
- **Odd one out / find the near synonym** (lexical retrieval).
- **Aural gap-fill**, **delayed dictation**, **listen and change** (switch tense or person).

## Sources (as given)
Krashen (1982); Richards (1983) micro-skills; Field (2008); Conti & Smith (2019) *Breaking the Sound Barrier*.

## Limits
Anecdotal; sub-skill drills should not replace meaning-focused listening.
