> Students don't fail at homework for lack of willpower so much as for vague tasks. A meaningful, specific, short task removes the need for self-control; McGonigal adds that self-control starts with noticing how you lose it.

## From the books
- Ahrens: a meaningful, well-defined task beats willpower; remove the temptation rather than resisting it.
- McGonigal (*The Willpower Instinct*): self-knowledge of how we lose control is the foundation of self-control; a short daily practice beats a long one postponed; self-control predicts academic success better than IQ (her claim, from Duckworth & Seligman 2005, *Claude*).

## Homework design *(Claude)*
- Specific output, size and time: "Record a 60-second answer to Part 2 card 14", not "practise speaking".
- Daily 10 minutes > weekly hour (spacing, too).
- Environment: phone in another room (see Oakley).
