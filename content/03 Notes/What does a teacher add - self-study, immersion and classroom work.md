> Plenty of people learn Russian, Turkish or English from films and YouTube with no teacher. If learning can happen through self-study and immersion, a teacher's value lies in what those routes don't give: selection, sequencing, feedback, accountability and pushed output.

## A working answer *(Claude)*
- **Selection and sequencing** of input at the right level (i+1, coverage).
- **Feedback** that makes gaps noticeable (CF, reformulation).
- **Pushed output** and interaction with consequences (Swain, Long).
- **Accountability and motivation**: structure, deadlines, a relationship.
- **Strategy training**: teaching learners to use self-study and immersion better (extensive listening, SRS, shadowing).

## Use it
- A strong opening for a YouTube video or first lesson: "Why pay a teacher?" then show these five.
