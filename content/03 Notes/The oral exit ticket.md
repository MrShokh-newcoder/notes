> End the lesson at the door: each child answers one question about the lesson before leaving. With very young learners it became an anticipated, favourite routine.

## Question stems by level
- "Tell me one thing / one word / two words from today."
- "What can you see in this flashcard?"
- "How do you say … in English?"
- "What did you like / learn today?"
- Older learners: written 3-2-1, one open question, or a one-sentence summary.

## Limits
One turn per child; time cost in big classes; no outcome data.
