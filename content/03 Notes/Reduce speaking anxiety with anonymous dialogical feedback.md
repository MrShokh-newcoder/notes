> For many learners, anxiety comes from feeling unable to express themselves, not from specific language items. A weekly anonymous written exchange (the learner writes how they feel; the teacher replies with one strategy tip and encouragement) reportedly lowered tension.

## Routine
1. End of lesson: anonymous slips on how today felt, or how a phrase or task went.
2. The teacher replies in writing to each slip: one learning-strategy tip + encouragement; returned next lesson.
3. Low-confidence or low-level learners: offer answer templates ("Good? Bad? So-so?") before "Why?".

## Mitigation menu (from the literature)
Relaxed atmosphere · small-group work · behavioural contracts · journals · slower pacing · instructions for homework and tests in L1 or in writing. Refer severe cases to specialists.

## Theory
Horwitz, Horwitz & Cope (1986) *MLJ* 70 (Foreign Language Classroom Anxiety); Scovel (1978); MacIntyre (1999); Dörnyei & Ryan (2015); King (2014); Oxford (2006).

## Limits
Anecdotal; Japanese adult and university classes.
