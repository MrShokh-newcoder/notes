> Learning to learn can start very young and belongs inside lessons, not as an extra: self-knowledge, metacognitive, cognitive and socio-affective strategies, and attitudes like perseverance.

## Moves (from 12 suggestions)
- Strategy training: model → explain → practise → transfer.
- End-of-lesson and end-of-unit reviews; learner diaries; self-assessment.
- Class discussion and "conferencing" about learning.
- Negotiated class contract.
- Mind maps for vocabulary and planning.
- Pre-evaluation (what I know, will learn, how) and post-evaluation.
- Dictionary and reference skills.
- "Equipping them to become competent and confident life-long learners is one of the most important things."

## Limits (from comments)
Gets squeezed out in upper primary by syllabus pressure. Reviewing in L2 is hard, so allow L1 for reflection.
