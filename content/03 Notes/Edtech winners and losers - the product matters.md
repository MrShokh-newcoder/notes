> Byju's, once valued at $22bn with a World Cup sponsorship and Messi as ambassador, collapsed: late accounts, mass lay-offs, a bankrupt US affiliate, investor write-offs. Kerr's lesson: product quality and pedagogy mattered less to the company than investment logic, and that's what failed.

## Lessons for my projects *(Claude, from Kerr)*
- Judge platforms (including my own) by a sample lesson, not marketing.
- Sustainable unit economics with paying users over investor-funded growth.
- Reliability and pedagogy first; a buggy app loses trust fast.
- Avoid high-pressure sales to low-income families (the accusation reported against Byju's).

## Limits
A single case from press reports; no outcome data.
