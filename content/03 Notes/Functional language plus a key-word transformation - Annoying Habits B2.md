> A B2 conversation lesson on irritating habits that bundles a functional set (complaining about habits) with idioms in context and a B2 First Part 4 key-word transformation: discussion and exam practice from one theme.

## Structure
1. Lead-in: what's been getting on your nerves at home, in the street, on transport?
2. Structures for annoyance: *always* + present continuous; *keep* + -ing; *I wish you wouldn't…*; *It drives me crazy when…*; *I can't stand it when…*; *Would you mind + -ing?*
3. Four short texts about housemates; questions.
4. Idioms from context (*hog the bathroom, give the silent treatment*), then discussion.
5. Key-word transformation practice (2–5 words, keep the given word).

## Why it's a good model *(Claude)*
The functional set *is* the grammar FCE tests (*wish + would*, *keep + -ing*), so the transformation task recycles what was just used communicatively, instead of standing alone as test drill.

## Limits
Adult, no timings; most of the site is B2–C2 adult material (a small Young Learners category exists, unchecked).
