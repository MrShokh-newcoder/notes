> After a century of target-language-only orthodoxy, translation is back as a productive task. It's useful for lexis if three pitfalls are managed: word-for-word output, over-reliance on L1, and L1 gaps (words like *shallow*, *insight*, *challenge* without a neat equivalent).

## Activities
1. **Back translation**: English sentences → L1; a week later (or swapped), back to English and compare (*You're better off without him*; *I should really get going*).
2. **Give me the gist**: teacher reads a short L1 news item; learners summarise it in English.
3. **Revising the draft**: orally translate an L1 text, then improve it against an English text on the same topic (notice the gap).
4. **Alternative versions**: groups swap and improve each other's translations.
- To prevent word-for-word output, ask "How would you *say* this in English?", not "translate".
