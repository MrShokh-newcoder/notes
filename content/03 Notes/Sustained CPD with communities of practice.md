> British Council evaluations converge: one-off training doesn't stick. Sustained, practice-based CPD with communities of practice, teacher agency and accreditation works better.

## Indonesia evaluation (2025)
[Renandya et al.](https://www.teachingenglish.org.uk/publications/case-studies-insights-and-research/development-cpd-programme-models-service-primary): 486 teachers, 34 teacher educators. Reported gains in engagement, confidence, spoken English and student-centred methods (**including gamification**, PBL, storytelling); motivation came through Communities of Practice. Barriers: internet, time, workload, weak incentives. Needs analysis: many classrooms still teacher-centred.

## Scalable TPD (2026)
14 case studies: quality elements are collaborative design, expert-led curriculum-aligned content, inclusive delivery, teacher agency and efficacy, accreditation. Teacher Activity Groups are a low-cost, teacher-led format.

## Limits
Sponsor's own evaluations; no control groups; self-report.
