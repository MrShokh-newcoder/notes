> Telegram works on basic phones and low bandwidth. It supports both broadcast and interaction: channels for content, groups for discussion, live sessions with polls, quizzes and recordings, and no algorithmic filtering.

## Uses
- **Channel**: post resources, links, short audio and video.
- **Group**: discussion around the posted content.
- **Live sessions**: video/audio, screen share, polls, quizzes; record for revision.
- Multiple admins to moderate.

## Limits
2022 figures (participant caps, features) need re-checking. Privacy and data protection not addressed. Promotes his paid course.
