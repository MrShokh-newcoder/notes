> One folded A4 sheet makes an 8-page mini-book. It teaches children how books work and gives them a reading and writing object they're proud of, filled with recycled vocabulary and sentence frames.

## Make it
Teacher-scripted fold-and-tear demonstration (the instructions themselves are comprehensible input), ending in a cross-cut booklet. Credits Theresa Zanatta.

## Content frames
- "In my [colour] school bag there is a [colour] [object]."
- "My monster's got [number] [body part]." (numbers 1–10)
- "Rui! What are you doing?" (yes/no questions in the present continuous, ending "I'm reading.")
- "My little book of calm" (scribble, then write)

## Limits
Ages mostly unstated; the benefits are experiential.
